Personal Memory — Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Personal Memory is a private, personal-use integration operated by Juan Olivella to review his own WHOOP health and fitness information. It is not a public service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WHOOP.

Information and purpose

With the account holder’s authorization, the integration is intended to read WHOOP sleep, recovery, physiological cycle and workout records, including available heart rate, heart rate variability, strain and timing information. Any additional data access is limited by the permissions displayed in WHOOP’s authorization screen. The purpose is personal retrieval, activity summaries and understanding fitness patterns, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

Processing and storage

The integration is intended to run on the account holder’s computer. OAuth credentials and any downloaded records are kept outside this public website and its repository. When the account holder asks an AI assistant to review records, the selected information may be processed by that assistant’s provider under its applicable account settings and privacy terms. Local storage does not mean that information used in an AI conversation stays entirely on the device.

Sharing

This integration does not sell data or publish health records to GitHub. WHOOP supplies authorized records; an AI provider may process records used in a requested analysis. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary visitor connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement. The page has no custom analytics, advertising or data collection form.

Retention and control

The account holder controls local copies and can delete them and the integration’s stored credentials. Revoking the application in WHOOP stops further authorized access but does not automatically delete existing local copies or prior AI conversations. Those must be removed separately, subject to the relevant provider’s retention policies.

Contact and changes

The operator is the sole intended user. General questions about this policy may be submitted through the policy repository. Do not include health records, credentials or other private information in public issues. This policy will be updated if the integration’s data handling changes.
